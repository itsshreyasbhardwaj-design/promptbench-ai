# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward, be kind, and assume good faith. Critique the code, not the
person.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with reasons. "This will break under concurrent
  writes because…" is useful; "this is bad" is not.
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before concluding they
  were careless.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline your change, and that a contributor may
  decline your suggestion. Both are normal.
- Respect that people contribute in their own time, across time zones, in a
  language that may not be their first.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad-faith argument
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and code comments — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private security advisory or
contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits and issues that violate this document,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe
violations. Where a response is not obvious, maintainers will explain their
reasoning.
